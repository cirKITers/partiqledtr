# In-algebra encoding of pair invariants

An open theory question, split out of the phase-6 design (2026-08-31). Not scheduled:
whether the unflattening theory extends to encodings whose data gates are generated by
the ansatz's own algebra has to be settled before any implementation.

## The construction

The pair invariant `m_ij^2 = (p_i + p_j)^2` is the physical decision variable for the
LCA level, and it is a *pair-level* scalar the per-particle product encoding forces the
circuit to synthesize trigonometrically. The proposal: load it directly on the
cross-particle bonds, after the per-particle RY product layer,

    |psi(x)> = U_enc(m~) . prod_q RY(theta_q) |0>,

    U_enc(m~) = prod_{b in cross bonds} exp(-i (m~ / 2) (X_j X_k + Y_j Y_k)),

with `m~ = atan2(m_ij, s)` a bounded chart (scale `s` from the training split, in the
style of the pair-polar map). `X_j X_k` and `Y_j Y_k` commute on a bond, so the gate is
the XY coupling itself with the data as its angle: the encoding generator is an element
of `i g` for every arm whose bond set carries it.

## What the manuscript's own lemmas appear to give

Three observations, each one step from a lemma already in the paper. **Derived
2026-08-31 and not yet verified — checking them is the first task.**

1. **The input factor cannot see the pair invariant.** Every `U in e^g` acts on `i g`
   as a Hilbert-Schmidt isometry preserving each ideal (the adjoint-invariance lemma),
   so `P_{g_j}(U_enc rho U_enc^dag) = P_{g_j}(rho)` for every component and every value
   of `m~`. With the RY layer first, the g-purity entering the variance law is that of
   the RY product state alone: the closed forms survive verbatim, and the
   input-distribution question stays a statement about the per-particle angles.
2. **The annihilation survives.** At clustered per-particle angles the state is a
   computational basis state, and `U(theta) U_enc(m~)` is still generated by `g`, so
   the exact-zero identity applies at any depth and any `m~`. In-algebra injection is
   therefore **not** a rescue channel: it cannot replace the learned preconditioner,
   which keeps its role in every grid.
3. **The parameter ensemble is undisturbed.** The variance formula's 2-design
   assumption concerns the ensemble of `U(theta)`; multiplying by a fixed `U_enc`
   leaves an exact design invariant, and an approximate one approximately so.

If 1-3 hold, the construction is *spectral enrichment at zero cost — and zero benefit —
to the input factor*: the pair invariant enters the loss only through where the encoded
state's in-algebra projection points relative to the observable, i.e. through the mean,
not the variance.

## The questions that are actually open

1. What is the right trainability statement when the data enters through the mean? The
   variance law prices the input distribution through `P_g`; observation 1 says `m~`
   never touches it. Is there a data-resolved quantity — an alignment between
   `Ad_{U_enc}` applied to the projection of `rho` and the observable — that plays the
   role `P_g` plays for the product part?
2. Does the floor criterion generalise? Its factorization argument needs a product
   state, which `U_enc` destroys. Natural candidate: count basis strings diagonal in
   the eigenbasis of the *encoding generator set* rather than of a single-qubit Pauli.
   The manuscript's open question 1 already names site-dependent clustering bases; this
   is the entangled-basis version of it.
3. What spectrum does the bond gate add? `XX + YY` on a bond has eigenvalues
   `{-2, 0, 0, 2}`, so one pass contributes harmonics `{1, 2}` in `m~` — the same
   harmonics the trainable XY gates carry. Re-uploaded across layers this composes with
   the RY spectrum; the FCC (phase 5) is the natural descriptor for the joint object.
4. Re-uploading scope: with RY layers between trainable blocks, the intermediate
   purities are parameter-dependent (the manuscript's own caveat) *and* now
   data-dependent through the interleaved `U_enc`. Which statements survive?
5. Sanity check, expected trivial: the DLA closure is unchanged, since the encoding
   generators are in `g` — the hardness classification of the arm is untouched.

## Practical footnote

qml-essentials' `Encoding` handles single-qubit rotations; a two-qubit data gate would
be a new feature there (flag upstream per the project rule, no workaround). The layer
ordering also matters: observation 1 needs `U_enc` between the RY product layer and the
first trainable block, and qml-essentials orders layers ansatz-first (`RESEARCH.md`
§3). Neither is needed until the theory above says the construction is worth running.
